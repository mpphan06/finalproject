# HARGROVE MANOR
### A Murder in Five Perspectives

**Play here:** https://mpphan06.github.io/finalproject/

---

## ABOUT

Edmund Hargrove is dead. Your agency has been called in to investigate.
Move through the manor, gather evidence, and file your report — but you
only have time to collect so much before the day ends.

Every detective sees the case differently. Play through all five
perspectives to find the truth hidden beneath them.

---

## HOW TO PLAY

### Each Day

1. **Read the briefing** and review the suspect statements (📋 button,
   bottom right — available any time).

2. **Explore the manor.** Move freely between floors using the buttons
   on the right side of the screen.

3. **Collect evidence.** Click red-outlined clues to pick them up.
   You can only carry **4 pieces of evidence** per day.

   - 🔴 Red outline — unexamined clue
   - 🔵 Blue outline — dead end, won't help your case

4. **File your report.** Once all 4 slots are filled, click
   **File Report**, review your findings, and submit.

   - All 4 clues point to the same person → ending reached
   - Mixed or dead-end evidence → inconclusive, try again

5. **Return tomorrow.** Each successful day removes one piece of
   evidence from the house. Return and look again with fresh eyes.

---

### The Attic

### The Truth

After all five days are complete, you will be called to your desk.
Lay the evidence out. When you are ready — Reveal.
