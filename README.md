## HOW TO PLAY

**Goal:** Investigate the Hargrove manor across multiple days, accuse a
different suspect each day, and eventually piece together the true story.

---

### EACH DAY

1. **Read the briefing.** You can open the Suspect Statements folder at
   any time to review what each person told investigators.

2. **Explore the manor.** Move freely between floors using the floor
   buttons on the right side of the screen.

3. **Collect evidence.** Click on red-outlined clues to pick them up.
   You can only carry **4 pieces of evidence** per day — choose carefully.

   - **Red outline** = unexamined clue
   - **Blue outline** = dead end (won't help your case)
   - **Collected clues** appear in the evidence slots at the top of the screen

4. **File your report.** Once you have 4 pieces of evidence, the
   "File Report" button activates. Click it to review your evidence
   and submit your conclusion.

   - If all 4 clues point to the **same person** → you reach an ending
   - If your evidence is **split or includes a dead end** → inconclusive,
     try again (the day resets, no evidence is lost from the house)

5. **Return tomorrow.** Each successful day retains one key piece of
   evidence and removes it from the house permanently. The next day
   begins fresh with slightly fewer clues available.

---

### THE ATTIC

The attic on **Floor 3** is locked. The key is hidden somewhere on
**Floor 1**. Finding it lets you access a room that holds a different
kind of evidence.

---

### THE TRUTH

After completing all five suspect days, you will be taken to your
**office desk**. Review what you have gathered across all five days.
When you are ready, hit **Reveal**.

---

### CONTROLS & UI

| Element | Function |
|---|---|
| Floor buttons (right side) | Move between floors |
| 📋 button (bottom right) | Open suspect statements at any time |
| ♪ button | Mute / unmute music |
| Red-outlined clues | Click to pick up evidence |
| Blue-outlined clues | Dead ends — click to read why |
| File Report | Available once all 4 slots are filled |
| Reset All Progress | Starts the game over completely |
