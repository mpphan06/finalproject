CASE CLOSED

Inspector Aldous Croft

The detective assigned to the Hargrove case was found dead in their office that evening. The scene was staged to look like a self-inflicted wound. Inspector Croft signed off on the preliminary report before the body was cold.

The Hargrove case was officially closed. Unsolved.

Croft retired six months later with a commendation for distinguished service. No one asked questions. No one was left who knew enough to ask.

The five reports remain in the record. Five accusations. One truth. One body.
